## Suggestions for Further Exploration
### <i class="fa-solid fa-book"></i> Books
#### For Everybody

- [*Einstein for Beginners*](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0375714596/) by Michael McGuiness and Joseph Schwartz
- [*Black Holes &amp; Time Warps*](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0393312763/) by Kip S. Thorne
- [*Space, Time, and Gravity: The Theory of the Big Bang and Black Holes*](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0226870294/) by Robert M. Wald
- [*The Cartoon Guide to Physics*](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0062731009/) by Larry Gonick and Art Huffman

#### For the More Mathematically-Minded

- [*Gravitation*](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0716703440/) by Charles W. Misner, Kip S. Thorne, and John Archibald Wheeler
- [*A First Course in General Relativity*](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0521277035/) by Bernard F. Schutz
- [*Spacetime and Geometry: An Introduction to General Relativity*](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0805387323/) by Sean Carroll
- [*Gravity: An Introduction to Einstein's General Relativity*](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0805386629/) by James B. Hartle
- [*General Relativity*](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0226870332/) by Robert M. Wald

### <i class="fa-solid fa-link"></i> Links

- [Virtual Trips to Black Holes and Neutron Stars](http://antwrp.gsfc.nasa.gov/htmltest/rjn_bht.html) — A set of relativistically accurate movies showing what it would be like to fall into a black hole or a neutron star
- [Space Time Travel](http://www.spacetimetravel.org/) — A site similar to the last one, showing relativity's effects on real pictures.
- [Relativistic Optics at ANU](http://www.anu.edu.au/Physics/Searle/) — Relativistically accurate 3-D pictures and movies
- [gmunu@mit](http://gmunu.mit.edu) — Relativity at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology
- [superstringtheory.com](http://superstringtheory.com/) — A great site; like this one, but for superstring theory
- [Cambridge Relativity](http://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/gr/public/) — Cambridge University's General Relativity website
- [Spacetime Wrinkles](http://archive.ncsa.uiuc.edu/Cyberia/NumRel/NumRelHome.html) — The Numerical Relativity site for University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign's National Center for Supercomputing Applications
- [space.com](http://www.space.com/) — A nice site, updated daily with news about space and space exploration
- [Hubble's Black Holes](http://hubblesite.org/discoveries/black_holes/) — A playful site with interactive animations, good for grades 6 and up
- [NASA's "Universe" Site](http://universe.nasa.gov/) — A site highlighting NASA's explorations of the history and origins of our Universe
- [Stars, Galaxies, and Cosmology](http://csep10.phys.utk.edu/astr162/lect/index.html) — The website for Astronomy 162 at the University of Tennessee at Knoxville; an excellent, in-depth site for all areas of astronomy
- [LISA](http://lisa.nasa.gov/) — The Laser Interferometer Space Antenna
- [LIGO](http://www.ligo.org/science/overview.php) — The Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory
- [Einstein At Home](http://einstein.phys.uwm.edu/) — A public project for distributed computing; allows users to help find real gravitational waves with a screen saver
- [HyperPhysics](http://hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/hbase/hph.html) — A very useful site, with information from all areas of physics
- [The Center for Gravitational Wave Physics at Penn State University](http://cgwp.gravity.psu.edu/)
- [A photo album of images taken by the Hubble Space Telescope](http://hubblesite.org/gallery/album/)
- [The news center for the Hubble Space Telescope](http://hubblesite.org/newscenter/)
