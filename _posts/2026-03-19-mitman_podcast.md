# Keefe Mitman featured on Fraser Cain's podcast

A postdoctoral researcher in our collaboration, [Keefe Mitman](https://keefemitman.github.io/)
---a NASA Hubble Fellow at Cornell University, was recently featured in a Youtube video by
journalist Fraser Cain: [Everything You Need to Know About Gravitational Waves in 2026](https://youtu.be/_jmQVH9L8vI?si=l1O_x-cYo03bDKR-). In the video, Keefe and Fraser
discuss recent progress by the LVK gravitational-wave detectors as well as the
potential future of gravitational-wave science and everything that it has to offer.
