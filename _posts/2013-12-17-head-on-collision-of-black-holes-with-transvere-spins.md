# Head on Collision of Black Holes with Transvere Spins

The spins of the black holes are transverse to the infall direction,
anti-aligned and of magnitude 0.5. This simulation is described in a
publication
[*Phys. Rev. D*](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.82.064031),
also available at
[[arXiv:gr-qc/0907.0869](http://arxiv.org/abs/0907.0869)].
