# Mark Scheel elected to Fellow of the American Physical Society

We're so happy that Mark Scheel, one of the senior members of the SXS
collaboration, has been elected a 2024 Fellow of the American Physical Society!

{% include image.html class="right"
   url="/images/Scheel_250.jpg"
   description="Mark Scheel"
%}

The citation for his fellowship reads:

> For major contributions to numerical relativity, including being a
> principal author of the Spectral Einstein Code (SpEC), applying SpEC
> to develop highly accurate surrogate gravitational waveform models,
> and leading the development of the Cauchy-Characteristic Extraction
> code.

Congratulations to Mark!
