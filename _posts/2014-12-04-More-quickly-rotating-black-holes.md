# More quickly rotating black holes

The SXS collaboration can now simulate binaries containing black holes
that spin at a rate of 99.4% of their maximum theoretical value, the
fastest spins ever simulated.  The technical improvements allowing
such large spins are reported at
[[arXiv:1412.1803](http://arxiv.org/abs/1412.1803)] and an
investigation of the maximum spin during binary coalescence can be
found at [[arXiv:1411.7297](http://arxiv.org/abs/1411.7297)].
