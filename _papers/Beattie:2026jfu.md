abstract: |
  Binary neutron star (BNS) mergers excite a Kelvin--Helmholtz
  instability (KHI) in the shear layer between them. It is parasitized
  upon by the magnetic field to form a powerful magnetic dynamo. Using
  a large ensemble of high-resolution visco-resistive KHI-driven
  dynamo simulations we construct analytical, asymptotic in Reynolds
  number (\(\rm{Re}\)) models for the magnetic field growth rates and
  mean vertical field dynamo onset times. Our models suggest that in
  the \(\rm{Re} \gg 1\) limit, the mean vertical field is seeded
  immediately, kinematic growth rates become asymptotic in
  \(\rm{Re}\), and the dynamo moves out of the kinematic stage on
  \(\mathcal{O}(0.1\,\rm{ms})\) timescales, readily attaining
  magnetar-strength fields during this transition. This suggests that
  most global BNS merger simulations are not time-resolving the
  correct KHI-driven dynamo regime.
