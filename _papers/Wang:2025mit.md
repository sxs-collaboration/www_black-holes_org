abstract: |
  Supermassive black hole (SMBH) binaries in gaseous and stellar
  environments are prime targets for next-generation space-based
  gravitational wave detectors. Yet, realistic accretion conditions
  under which these binary systems evolve are not fully understood. In
  this work, we demonstrate the hypermagnetized multi-phase nature of
  the surrounding accretion flow formed by large-scale feeding from a
  galaxy background. Our simulations indicate that the hypermagnetized
  circumbinary disk is eccentric and warped, hosting a hot gas core
  for a parsec-scale separated binary. We also observe collimated
  bipolar magnetic tower-like outflows launched from each SMBH.
