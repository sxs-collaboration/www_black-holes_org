abstract: |
  Adaptive mesh refinement is a widely adopted strategy for grid-based
  numerical simulation codes to mitigate the high computational cost
  of multi-length scale problems. A robust and reliable mesh
  refinement criterion with minimal requirement for problem-specific
  tuning is critical for efficient and accurate simulations. Motivated
  by the oscillation indicators used in the spectral methods
  literature, we develop a new mesh refinement criterion employing a
  5-point stencil for multi-dimensional magnetohydrodynamics
  simulations, essentially extending the conventional 3-point Löhner
  scheme. We implement the criterion in the astrophysical fluid
  dynamics code AthenaK and test it across a variety of benchmark
  problems in Newtonian, special relativistic, and general
  relativistic magnetohydrodynamics, demonstrating its robustness and
  versatility.
