abstract: |
  We present a novel proof of a formula of Casini and Huerta for the
  entanglement entropy of the ground state of non-interacting massless
  Dirac fermions in dimension one localized to (a union of) intervals
  and generalize it to the case of Rényi entropies. At first, we prove
  that these entropies are well-defined for non-intersecting
  intervals. This is accomplished by an inequality of Alexander V.
  Sobolev. Then we compute this entropy using a trace formula for
  Wiener–Hopf operators by Harold Widom. For intersecting intervals,
  we discuss an extended entropy formula of Casini and Huerta and
  support this with a proof for polynomial test functions (instead of
  entropy).
