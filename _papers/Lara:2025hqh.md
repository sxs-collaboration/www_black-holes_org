abstract: |
  Black holes beyond General Relativity may carry non-standard charges
  that impact their phenomenology. We study how the scalar charge that
  is induced by the scalar-Gauss-Bonnet coupling is affected by the
  presence of a nontrivial kinetic term $K(X)$. We discuss the
  corresponding kinetic screening in the asymptotically flat, static
  solution first. We then turn to the case where self-accelerating
  cosmology is driven by $K(X)$, finding that the time-dependence of
  the scalar field opens up the parameter space, turning the black-
  hole scalar charge from secondary to primary. We provide a stability
  analysis and a measure of the intensity of the kinetic screening
  from the quartic dispersion relation of the mixed scalar and
  gravitational modes.
