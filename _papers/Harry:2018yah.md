abstract: |
  Substrate-transferred single-crystal semiconductor heterostructures
  are effective as low-thermal-noise optical coatings for small beams.
  We discuss developing GaAs/AlGaAs coatings at a size scale relevant
  to interferometric gravitational wave detectors such as LIGO.
