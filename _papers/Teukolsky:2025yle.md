abstract: |
  The characteristic decomposition for GRMHD in the comoving frame of
  the fluid has been known for a long time. However, it has not been
  known in the coordinate frame of the simulation and in terms of the
  conserved variables evolved in typical numerical simulations. This
  paper applies the method of quasi-invertible transformations
  developed in Paper I to derive this decomposition. Among other
  benefits, this will allow us to use the most accurate known
  computational methods, such as full-wave Riemann solvers. The
  results turn out to be simpler than expected based on earlier
  attempts.
