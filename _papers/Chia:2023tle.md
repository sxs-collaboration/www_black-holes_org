abstract: |
  \We report results on the first matched-filtering search for
  binaries with compact objects having large tidal deformabilities in
  the LIGO-Virgo gravitational wave (GW) data. The tidal deformability
  of a body is quantified by the “Love number” <math
  display="inline"><mi mathvariant="normal">Λ</mi><mo>∝</mo><mo
  stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>r</mi><mo>/</mo><mi>m</mi><msup><mo
  stretchy="false">)</mo><mn>5</mn></msup></math>, where <math
  display="inline"><mi>r</mi><mo>/</mo><mi>m</mi></math> is the body’s
  (inverse) compactness. Due to its strong dependence on compactness,
  the <math display="inline"><mi mathvariant="normal">Λ</mi></math> of
  larger-sized compact objects can easily be many orders of magnitude
  greater than those of black holes and neutron stars, leaving phase
  shifts which are sufficiently large for these binaries to be missed
  by binary black hole (BBH) templated searches. In this paper, we
  conduct a search using inspiral-only waveforms with zero spins but
  finite tides, with the search space covering chirp masses <math
  display="inline"><mn>3</mn><msub><mi>M</mi><mo
  stretchy="false">⊙</mo></msub><mo>&lt;</mo><mi
  mathvariant="script">M</mi><mo>&lt;</mo><mn>15</mn><msub><mi>M</mi><mo
  stretchy="false">⊙</mo></msub></math> and effective tidal
  deformabilities <math
  display="inline"><msup><mn>10</mn><mn>2</mn></msup><mo>≲</mo><mover
  accent="true"><mi mathvariant="normal">Λ</mi><mo
  stretchy="false">˜</mo></mover><mo>≲</mo><msup><mn>10</mn><mn>6</mn></msup></math>.
  We find no statistically significant GW candidates. This null
  detection implies an upper limit on the merger rate of such binaries
  in the range <math display="inline"><mrow><mo
  stretchy="false">[</mo><mn>1</mn><mo>-</mo><mn>300</mn><mo
  stretchy="false">]</mo><mtext> </mtext><mtext> </mtext><msup><mrow><mi>Gpc</mi></mrow><mrow><mo>-</mo><mn>3</mn></mrow></msup><mtext> </mtext><msup><mrow><mi>year</mi></mrow><mrow><mo>-</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow></msup></mrow></math>,
  depending on <math display="inline"><mi
  mathvariant="script">M</mi></math> and <math display="inline"><mover
  accent="true"><mi mathvariant="normal">Λ</mi><mo
  stretchy="false">˜</mo></mover></math>. While our constraints are
  model agnostic, we discuss the implications on beyond the Standard
  Model scenarios that give rise to boson stars and superradiant
  clouds. Using inspiral-only waveforms, we recover many of the BBH
  signals which were previously identified with full inspiral-merger-
  ringdown templates. We also empirically constrain the Love number of
  the compact objects in these binaries to <math display="inline"><mi
  mathvariant="normal">Λ</mi><mo>≲</mo><msup><mn>10</mn><mn>3</mn></msup></math>
  at the 90% credible level in the best cases, consistent with the
  expectation of vanishing Love number for black holes in general
  relativity. Our work is the first-ever dedicated template-based
  search for compact objects that are not black holes or neutron
  stars. Additionally, our work demonstrates a novel way of finding
  new physics in GW data, widening the scope of potential discovery to
  previously unexplored parameter space.
