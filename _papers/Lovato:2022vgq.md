abstract: |
  Since the release of the 2015 Long Range Plan in Nuclear Physics,
  major events have occurred that reshaped our understanding of
  quantum chromodynamics (QCD) and nuclear matter at large densities,
  in and out of equilibrium. The US nuclear community has an
  opportunity to capitalize on advances in astrophysical observations
  and nuclear experiments and engage in an interdisciplinary effort in
  the theory of dense baryonic matter that connects low- and high-
  energy nuclear physics, astrophysics, gravitational waves physics,
  and data science
