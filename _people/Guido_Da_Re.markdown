##### Research profile

My research centers on gravitational-wave extraction from binary black hole
simulations. I developed a model of the Bondi–Metzner–Sachs (BMS)
transformation that relates the inspiral and remnant (post-merger) frames of a
binary black hole merger, and I've been working on the development of the correct
initial data for Cauchy-characteristic evolution (CCE) in the
[SpECTRE](https://github.com/sxs-collaboration/spectre) code. In the future I
plan to extend this work to Cauchy-characteristic matching (CCM) to improve
waveform accuracy for future gravitational-wave detectors.

##### Biography

I am a PhD candidate at Caltech, working with Prof. Saul A. Teukolsky. I earned

both from the University of Trieste, where I was a student of the Collegio
Universitario di Merito Luciano Fonda.
