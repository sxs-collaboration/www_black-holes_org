##### Research Projects
Waveform decomposition techniques for scalable surrogate modeling of eccentric binary black hole waveforms, their analytical (post-Newtonian) and phenomenological modeling; Bayesian inference using eccentric waveform templates

##### Biography
<div style="text-align: justify;">
    <p>
        I am an Integrated PhD research scholar in the <a href="https://www.icts.res.in/research/astrorel">Astrophysics and Relativity group</a> at the <a href="https://icts.res.in/">International Centre for Theoretical Sciences (ICTS)</a>, Bangalore, India. Before joining ICTS, I was a Physics undergrad at <a href="https://www.ststephens.edu/">St. Stephen’s College</a>, New Delhi, India.
    </p>
    <p>
        My research focuses on modeling gravitational waveforms from black hole binaries in eccentric orbits using post-Newtonian, phenomenological, and surrogate modeling approaches. In particular, I am developing computationally efficient, scalable, and hardware-accelerable eccentric surrogate waveform models to support precision gravitational-wave astronomy of dynamically assembled binaries at scale using the current as well as the future generation of GW observatories. I am also hunting for eccentric black hole binaries in the LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA (LVK) data using Bayesian parameter estimation with eccentric waveform templates.
    </p>
</div>
