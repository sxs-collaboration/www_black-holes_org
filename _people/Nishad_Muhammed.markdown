##### Research Profile

I work on [SpEC](https://www.black-holes.org/for-researchers/spec). I have studied dynamical stability of hypermassive neutron stars produced after binary neutron star mergers. Currently I am studying energy release from neutrino-antineutrino annihilation in accretion disks formed by binary neutron star mergers and collapsed stars, which could be responsible for gamma-ray bursts.

##### Biography

I am a PhD candidate at Washington State University, working under the supervision of Dr. Matthew Duez. I received my BS in Physics from University of Dhaka. Before starting my current position, I received my MS working with Dr. Gaurav Khanna in Gravitational Wave Physics at Umass Dartmouth.
