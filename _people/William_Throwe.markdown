Core developer of SpECTRE.  Time-integration of PDEs.  Gravitational lensing

##### Biography

I received BSs in physics and mathematics from MIT in 2010, with a bachelor's
thesis on simulating extreme-mass-ratio inspirals with Scott Hughes.  My PhD
was given by Cornell in 2019 for work with Saul Teukolsky on SpECTRE, with
particular focus on improved time-integration techniques using local
time-stepping.  I have continued at Cornell on similar projects since then.
