##### Research Projects

Expansion of binary black hole parameter space to higher mass ratios. Accuracy of numerical relativity.

##### Biography

I am currently a PhD researcher under the supervision of Prof. Harald Pfeiffer at the Max Plank Institute for Gravitational Physics (Albert Einstein Institute) in Potsdam, Germany. Before this, I completed my BSc(Hons) in 2022 and MSc in 2024 at the University of Canterbury. My research concerns the accuracy of numerical relativity simulations, and producing accurate waveforms in more extreme regions of the parameter space, particularly with high mass ratio.
