##### Research Projects

I focus on modeling electromagnetic transients associated with compact objects
and their interactions with surrounding environment via magnetic or hydrodynamic
processes. I have worked on MHD and shock-capturing modules of
[SpECTRE](https://github.com/sxs-collaboration/spectre).


##### Biography

I received a BS in physics from Seoul National University in 2020, then received
a PhD in physics from Caltech in 2025 under the supervision of Elias Most and
Saul Teukolsky. Currently a PCTS-PGI joint postdoctoral fellow at Princeton
University.
