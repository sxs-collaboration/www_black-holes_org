##### Biography

I am an Associate Professor of Mathematics at UMass Dartmouth. Previously, I was a research associate at Cornell University and a Joint Space-Science Institute postdoctoral fellow at the University of Maryland. In 2011 I completed my PhD under the supervision of Jan Hesthaven at Brown University.
