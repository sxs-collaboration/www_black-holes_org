##### Research Projects

Numerical modelling of generic binary black holes

##### Biography

My name is Antoni Ramos-Buades and I am a Beatriz de Galindo distinguished professor at the University of the Balearic Islands (UIB) in Mallorca, Spain. Prior to this, I was a Veni fellow at the Dutch National Institute of Subatomic Physics (Nikhef) in Amsterdam, a postdoctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics (Albert Einstein Institute) in Potsdam, Germany, and an undergrad at UIB.

My research focuses on the numerical modelling of binary black holes in generic orbits. These systems with non-negligible orbital eccentricity provide invaluable information about their origin and their astrophysical formation processes. To explore these systems, I employ a range of methods, including Numerical Relativity and effective-one-body and phenomenological techniques to produce ready-to-use waveform models for gravitational-wave astronomy.
