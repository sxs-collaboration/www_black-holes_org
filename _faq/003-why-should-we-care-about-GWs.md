---
question: Why should we care about gravitational waves?
group: Gravitational Waves
---
Gravitational waves are predicted by Einstein's theory — the
fundamental theory of gravity that everything else in astronomy relies
on — but have not yet been observed. We need to know if Einstein's
theory is really right before we can make strong claims about the
nature of the Universe. If the theory is indeed correct, finding
gravitational waves will give us new insights into the theory, and
will give us powerful new tools observe the Universe.
