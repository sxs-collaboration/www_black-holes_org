---
question: I thought nothing could escape a black hole, so how does it evaporate to nothing?
group: Black Holes
---
This was the surprising discovery made by Stephen Hawking. Nothing can
escape a Black Hole, only if General Relativity is completely
correct. It turns out that GR is *not<* completely correct, just
mostly correct. If we include quantum mechanical effects, we find that
some things can escape.
