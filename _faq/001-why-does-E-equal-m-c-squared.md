---
question: Why does E = mc<sup>2</sup>?
group: Relativity
---
This famous formula was Einstein's most intriguing
prediction. Einstein made two assumptions: (1) all motion is relative
— you can't be moving unless you're moving *relative* to something
else; (2) everyone measures the same speed of light. With these two
assumptions, and a little math, Einstein proved that the only way
these two assumptions could be consistent with each other is if the
energy *E* of a resting body with mass *m* is $$mc^2$$.
