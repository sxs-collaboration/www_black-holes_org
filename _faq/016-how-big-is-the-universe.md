---
question: How big is the Universe?
group: Big Bang
---
We don't know. We know that it's been around for at least 10 billion
years, and the light in our Universe has been traveling since
then. Thus, if the "edge" of the Universe exists, it is at least 10
billion light-years away from us. It's possible that the Universe is
bigger than that, though — maybe even infinitely large!
