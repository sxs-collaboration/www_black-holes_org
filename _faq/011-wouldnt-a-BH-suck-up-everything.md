---
question: Wouldn't a Black Hole "suck up" everything in the Universe?
group: Black Holes
---
Not at all. Far outside a Black Hole, gravity feels just the same as
it would if the Hole were a regular star. For example, if our Sun were
suddenly replaced by a Black Hole with the same mass as the Sun, the
Earth would continue orbiting as usual (though things would get pretty
cold). The Sun's replacement would actually trap fewer things floating
through space than the Sun would, though they would stay trapped in a
black hole.
