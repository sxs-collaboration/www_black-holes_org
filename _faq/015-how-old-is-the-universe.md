---
question: How old is the Universe?
group: Big Bang
---
Current models of the Universe's past, together with observations
indicate that the Universe is roughly 14 billion years old. Now, we
know that our models aren't exactly correct, but they are pretty close
to the correct answer. That means that there is a little wiggle room
in this number, but it's pretty nearly right. In fact, astronomers
have seen stars that are at least 10 billion years old.
