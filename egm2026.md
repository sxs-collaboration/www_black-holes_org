**Dates:** May 28-29, 2026

**Location:** Cornell University, Ithaca, NY

---

The Eastern Gravity Meeting is an annual regional conference for
researchers at all career stages working in gravitational physics. The
meeting covers classical, quantum, theoretical, observational, and
computational approaches to the study of gravity. It is open to
participants from the Northeastern region and beyond.

## Registration and Abstract Submission

Registration is free but required. Abstracts may be submitted using
the form below.

**Abstract submission deadline:** May 8, 2026 (5:00 PM EDT)

[Register and submit an abstract](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScP5d2YSaGea7StgIfQynpS9YnLhnIKwzG2FRMxb3OfgQisIg/viewform?usp=dialog)

Each presenter will have **15 minutes** total (12 minutes for the talk
+ 3 minutes for questions).  There will be a DGRAV prize for the best
student talk.
