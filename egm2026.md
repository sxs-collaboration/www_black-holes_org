**Dates:** May 28-29, 2026

**Location:** Schwartz Auditorium,
[Rockefeller Hall, Cornell University, Ithaca, NY](https://maps.app.goo.gl/CteRa634ywurq8VeA)

---

The Eastern Gravity Meeting is an annual regional conference for
researchers at all career stages working in gravitational physics. The
meeting covers classical, quantum, theoretical, observational, and
computational approaches to the study of gravity. It is open to
participants from the Northeastern region and beyond.

## Registration and Abstract Submission

Registration is free but required. Abstracts may be submitted using
the form below.

**Abstract submission deadline:** May 8, 2026 (5:00 PM EDT)

[Register and submit an abstract](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScP5d2YSaGea7StgIfQynpS9YnLhnIKwzG2FRMxb3OfgQisIg/viewform?usp=dialog)

We anticipate that each presenter will have **15 minutes** total (12 minutes for
the talk plus 3 minutes for questions).  There will be a DGRAV prize for the
best student talk.

## Travel

- Ithaca has a local airport
  [https://www.flyithaca.com/](https://www.flyithaca.com/) The airport has a red
  phone near the exit that allows you to contact a taxi.
- There are also airports within an hour drive in Syracuse, Elmira, and
  Binghamton.
- Many bus lines service Ithaca, including Grey Hound, FlixBus, and Cornell also
  runs a bus to/from NYC.
- It's a beautiful drive to Ithaca!

## Local transit

- Most hotels in Ithaca offer a free shuttle service to and from Cornell. Likely
  the easiest dropoff and pickup location should be Clark Hall.
- Ithaca also has an excellent bus system, [Tompkins Consolidated Area
  Transit](https://tcatbus.com/). You can use tap-to-pay to pay with a credit
  card directly. Fares are $1.50.
- There are very few to no ride share services like Lyft and Uber in Ithaca. You
  can easily wait an hour if you try to use those.
- Several taxi companies, like [College Town Cab](https://collegetowncab.com/)
  operate in Ithaca and are quite reliable.
- [Cornell has some on-campus parking](https://www.cornell.edu/visit/parking/)

## Accommodations

- The [Statler Hotel](https://statlerhotel.cornell.edu/) is a very nice hotel
  run by the Cornell Hotel School.
- The [Best
  Western](https://www.bestwestern.com/en_US/book/hotels-in-ithaca/best-western-university-inn/propertyCode.33065.html)
  is reasonably priced and runs a shuttle.
- The [Dorm Hotel](https://thedormhotel.com/) is about as close as possible,
  with a short walk up hill.
- [Hotel Ithaca](https://www.thehotelithaca.com/) is conveniently located near
  the Ithaca Commons near many restaurants.
- [Cayuga Blu Hotel](https://cayugabluhotel.com/) is right on bust route 30, but
  is a bit more isolated. However, there are several good restaurants nearby.
